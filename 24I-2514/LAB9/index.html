Task 3: Flight Route Network

Dataset: flights.csv

Force-directed node-link diagram of weekly flights between Pakistani cities. Node radius encodes route degree, line width encodes flight frequency. Drag nodes or hover over a city to highlight connections.

Node Radius: Route Degree (Number of Connections) Link Width: Flights per Week (6 – 70)

Task 5: Pakistan Cities Point Map

Datasets: pakistan_cities.csv & custom.geo.json

Mercator projection map centered on Pakistan. Cities are marked with semi-transparent circles whose area represents population. Only cities with population > 1 Million are labeled.

Task 6: Combined Spatial Encoding Map

Datasets: pakistan_cities.csv & flights.csv

Integrated map combining cities, populations, and air corridors with four simultaneous spatial encodings. Hover over a city to highlight its routes.

1. Position: Lat/Lon Location 2. Size: Population (Circle Area) 3. Color: Hub (≥4 routes) Non-Hub (<4 routes) 4. Line Width: Flights per Week
